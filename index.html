About AFX

Seamless Logistics Exceptional Results

One World, One Freight — AFX (Arabian Freight Xpress). At AFX, we are committed to delivering world-class logistics and supply chain management services to businesses across a wide range of industries.

Headquartered in the heart of Dubai, UAE, with a dedicated presence in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia, we offer comprehensive and customized solutions for air cargo, sea cargo, GCC land transportation, warehousing, customs brokerage, animal transport, and more.

With a strong and reliable presence across the GCC, USA, UK, China, Thailand, and India, AFX is equipped to handle global demands — ensuring your cargo moves faster, safer, and smarter, anywhere in the world.
