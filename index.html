About AFX — من نحن

Seamless Logistics Exceptional Results

خدمات لوجستية سلسة — نتائج استثنائية

One World, One Freight — AFX (Arabian Freight Xpress). At AFX, we are committed to delivering world-class logistics and supply chain management services to businesses across a wide range of industries.

Headquartered in Dubai, UAE, with a dedicated office and warehouse in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia, we offer comprehensive solutions for شحن جوي (air cargo), شحن بحري (sea freight), نقل بري (GCC land transport), مستودعات (warehousing), تخليص جمركي (customs brokerage), and more.

With a strong and reliable presence across the GCC, USA, UK, China, Thailand, and India, AFX is equipped to handle global freight demands — ensuring your cargo moves faster, safer, and smarter.
